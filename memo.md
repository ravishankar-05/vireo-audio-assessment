To: Priya Raman, Head of Customer Experience
Subject: Q3 Training Budget & Root Cause of CSAT Drop

The Bottom Line:
We should halt the ₹4 Lakh agent training initiative. The CSAT drop is not an agent performance issue; it is a hardware defect issue tied to the recent festive volume.

By joining the helpdesk tickets with Finance's order export, I isolated the root cause. Lots PL2-2510 and PL2-2511 of the VA-EB-PL2 earbuds are failing at an unprecedented rate.

The overall CSAT is 3.33, but the CSAT for these specific lots has plummeted to 2.73.

Agents handling all other products are maintaining a healthy 3.39 CSAT.

Financial Impact:
Using the cost standards from the Support Policy (Unit cost + ₹340 logistics), these defective lots have cost the company roughly ₹16,08,421 in replacements, refunds, and support agent time (at ₹290 per contact).

Recommendation:
Do not retrain the agents. Escalating this defect to the Product team and recalling or stopping sales of the remaining PL2 October/November lots will eliminate the ticket volume at the source, instantly improving CSAT and stopping the operational bleed.
